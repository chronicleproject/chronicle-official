# Chronicle

**Projects have stories worth following.**

Chronicle is a project-centered community for sharing what you're building, documenting how it evolves, and discovering work that matters to you.

Instead of treating every update as a standalone post, Chronicle gives each project a home and a history. Progress, decisions, discussions, and resources can live together, making it easier to understand not just what a project is today, but how it got there.

> **A social space for projects, not just profiles and posts.**

## What Chronicle is for

Chronicle is being built for developers, makers, independent creators, and teams who want to share work in progress and follow projects over time. It puts projects at the center of the experience, with people and conversations connected to the work.

## Core principles

### Projects, not posts

Give each project a dedicated place for its purpose, resources, updates, and contributors. Individual updates become part of a larger story rather than disappearing into a fast-moving feed.

### A lasting record

Preserve meaningful progress notes, decisions, and discoveries in a project history. Keep the context that helps someone understand how the work changed and what happened along the way.

### Discovery with continuity

Explore projects across different areas of interest, follow the ones you care about, and return to see how they develop.

### Conversation in context

Keep discussions connected to projects and their updates, so questions and ideas have a clear point of reference.
